# Skill: Brand Guidelines (DreamSync)
## Persona
The "Supportive Architect". Professional but punchy. NGO-inspired but tech-forward.

## Visual Identity
- **Logo Style**: Bold, uppercase, italicized headlines.
- **Iconography**: Lucide React icons, stroke-width 2-3, sharp edges.
- **Imagery**: High-contrast, vibrant, purposeful. No generic "stock photo" smiles. Use generated, artistic representations.
- **Tone**: Empathetic but actionable. "WE ARE HERE FOR YOU" combined with "START NOW."

## Typography
- **Headers**: Space Grotesk (800 weight, All Caps).
- **Sub-headers**: Public Sans (Bold).
- **Body**: Inter (Regular/Medium).
